Setup Guide

Windows SmartScreen Notice

Because SINOP is a brand-new, independently created teacher application without high-cost corporate code-signing certificates, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a blue warning screen. Follow these simple steps:

1

Open Installer

Double-click the downloaded SINOP-Setup-1.4.5.exe file in your Downloads folder.

2

Click "More Info"

When the blue window appears stating "Windows protected your PC", click the small underline link that says More info.

3

Click "Run Anyway"

A new button titled Run anyway will appear at the bottom right. Click it to launch the fast installation!

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about installing, using, and managing SINOP.

Does SINOP require an active internet connection?
SINOP is designed as an offline-first desktop application. All your student records, school forms, test items, classroom utilities, and generators run locally on your computer without needing Wi-Fi or mobile data. Internet is only required for three things: verifying your license key, generating AI-powered lesson plans (which use your free Google Gemini API key), and optionally syncing backups to your private Google Drive folder or checking for software updates. Everything else works completely offline.
Is SINOP really free for teachers?
SINOP offers a full 7-day free trial with zero restrictions so you can evaluate everything before committing. After the trial, affordable plans are available: ₱99/month or ₱350/year (save over 70%). These modest fees help maintain ongoing development, server costs for license verification, and continuous feature updates for the teacher community.
How does Google Drive Cloud Backup work?
Cloud backup is completely optional. If connected, SINOP creates a dedicated isolated folder in your Google Drive named "SINOP Cloud Files" where encrypted archive backups (.zip) are saved. SINOP never scans or accesses your personal photos or documents.
What are the system requirements?
Windows: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit), 4 GB RAM minimum, 500 MB free disk space.
Mac: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later (Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3 or Intel).
