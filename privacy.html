Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 • Last Updated: September 13, 2026

SINOP ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an offline-first desktop productivity and classroom management application created for educators. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of the application.

This Privacy Policy outlines how SINOP handles your data, with specific focus on our optional Google Drive Cloud Backup integration.

1. Offline-First Architecture & Local Data Storage

SINOP is engineered with a strict offline-first philosophy:

  • All student rosters, grades, test evaluations, lesson plans, and classroom documents are stored strictly locally on your computer inside an encrypted database.
  • We do not operate central database servers that collect or monitor your school records.
  • Your personal and classroom information never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

2. Google Drive API & Cloud Backup Integration

SINOP provides an optional Cloud Backup tool allowing users to securely archive and restore their workspace.

When you choose to link your Google Account, SINOP requests the following minimum scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Grants access only to files and folders created or opened by SINOP.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email: Used solely to display the currently connected Google account email in the app interface.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile: Used solely to display your profile display name and avatar within the backup interface.
Folder Isolation Guarantee: SINOP only manages its own dedicated folder titled "SINOP Cloud Files" and the backup archives (.zip) placed inside it. SINOP never accesses, reads, scans, modifies, or deletes your personal files, documents, photos, or other items in your Google Drive.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

SINOP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data or Google Drive data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, third-party data brokers, or external analytics firms.
  • We do not use your data or Google Drive contents for training artificial intelligence (AI) models.

5. Local Credential Storage & Security

All OAuth tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) received from Google during authorization are stored locally on your own computer within the application's secure user data directory. These tokens are used exclusively by your local installation to communicate directly with Google's official API endpoints and are never transmitted to any third-party server.

6. User Control, Disconnection & Data Deletion

You have full and continuous control over your connected account and backup data:

  • Disconnect at any time: You can disconnect your Google Account inside the application via the Cloud Resources panel. Disconnecting immediately wipes all stored tokens from your device.
  • Revoke Permissions via Google: You can revoke SINOP’s access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Permissions Page.
  • Delete Backups: You can delete backup archives from within SINOP or directly inside your Google Drive folder at any time.
