Chapter 1

Getting Started & Installation

SINOP is a 100% offline desktop application built specifically for Filipino educators. It runs natively on your laptop or desktop computer with zero cloud dependency for core features.

System Requirements

Before installing, ensure your personal laptop or DepEd-issued computer satisfies the following lightweight requirements:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit architecture) or macOS 12 Monterey or later.
  • Memory (RAM): 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended for seamless multi-tab multitasking).
  • Disk Space: Approximately 500 MB free storage for application files and local encrypted SQLite database.
  • Display Resolution: Minimum 1366 × 768 pixels (optimized for standard DepEd laptop screens).

Bypassing Windows SmartScreen (First Launch)

Because SINOP is an independently developed teacher application and does not purchase high-cost enterprise certificates from Microsoft, Windows Defender SmartScreen may show a blue notice dialog on initial installation. This is completely normal and safe.

1

Download and Launch

Download the latest verified installer (SINOP-Setup-1.X.X.exe) and double-click to run.

2

Click "More Info"

When the blue window appears stating "Windows protected your PC", click the small underline link that says More info.

3

Click "Run Anyway"

A button labeled Run anyway will appear at the bottom right. Click it to begin installation.

Clean Installation: SINOP installs automatically without bundling any 3rd-party adware, toolbars, or tracking telemetry. A desktop shortcut is automatically created for quick daily access.

How to Activate Your License Key

When launching SINOP for the first time, the onboarding setup wizard welcomes you with a 2-step registration:

1

Enter Your Nickname

Type your preferred name (e.g. Teacher MJ or Teacher Sarah). This name will be displayed in greetings, dashboard widgets, and certificates.

2

Input License Key & 4-Digit Security PIN

Paste your product key (formatted as SINOP-XXXX-XXXX). Below the key, enter a 4-digit Security PIN (e.g. 1234).

Why the 4-Digit PIN matters: If your laptop breaks or you switch to a new school computer, this PIN allows you to verify ownership and self-transfer your active license without waiting for technical support!

Quick Action Buttons on Onboarding

At the bottom of the onboarding screen, 4 dedicated action icons allow you to manage your license instantly:

  • Contact Support (Chat Icon): Direct links to Teacher MJ's official Facebook, TikTok (@teachermj_), and email support.
Chapter 3

Connecting Your Free AI API Key

SINOP features an advanced AI Lesson Plan Engine that generates DepEd-compliant Daily Lesson Logs (DLL) in seconds. To power this engine, you connect your own free AI API key.

Google Gemini is 100% Free: Google AI Studio provides free personal API keys with generous daily limits—more than enough to generate all your weekly lesson plans, quizzes, and worksheets at zero monthly cost!

How to Get Your Free Google Gemini API Key (3 Minutes)

1

Visit Google AI Studio

Open your browser and navigate to aistudio.google.com/api-keys. Log in with your DepEd Google Workspace or personal Gmail account.

2

Create API Key

Click the blue button titled Create API Key. Choose "Create API key in new project".

3

Copy the Key String

A string starting with AIzaSy... will appear. Click Copy to copy it to your clipboard.

4

Paste in SINOP

In SINOP, click your avatar at the bottom-left sidebar → API Secrets (or when prompted inside the Lesson Planner). Select Google Gemini (Recommended) and paste your key.

Multi-Provider Support: Although Google Gemini is the free default, SINOP also supports OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic Claude, and DeepSeek if you prefer using custom developer models.

Chapter 7

School Forms & Attendance (SF2)

Eliminate manual clerical computation errors with automated School Form 2 attendance summaries, baseline enrollments, and printable parent notice letters.

SINOP — School Form 2 (SF2) Attendance Matrix Click to Zoom
Figure 7.1: 5-Week calendar-aligned SF2 attendance matrix with automated monthly summaries.

Official DepEd Attendance Marks

Click any student cell to toggle official DepEd attendance indicators:

  • Blank: Present
  • X: Absent (Unexcused)
  • L: Tardy / Late
  • C: Cutting Classes
  • AM or PM: Half-Day Absence (automatically computed as 0.5 in denominators)
  • NOCLASS / Holiday: Click the column date header to toggle non-instructional days. The app deducts non-class days from attendance formulas automatically.
5-Day Consecutive Absence Notice Letters: Whenever a student reaches 5 consecutive unexcused absences, SINOP automatically triggers an alert and generates a ready-to-print DepEd Parent Notice / Home Visitation letter complete with student details and detachable parent acknowledgement slip!

Chapter 9

Data Privacy & Hardware Migration

Your school data belongs strictly to you. SINOP enforces strict offline-first data protection and provides self-service hardware migration.

Local Encrypted SQLite Storage

All student records, LRNs, grades, anecdotal notes, and lesson plans are stored locally on your device inside an encrypted SQLite database (sinop_enc.db). TMJ Classroom Solutions does not operate central company servers that collect or harvest your school records.

Transferring to a New Laptop (Self-Service Migration)

If you upgrade your computer or change laptops:

  1. Install SINOP on your new computer from the official website.
  2. Enter your existing License Key (SINOP-XXXX-XXXX).
  3. When the screen notifies you that the key is currently bound to another device, enter your 4-digit Security PIN.
  4. Click Transfer License. The previous laptop is safely unbound and your new laptop is instantly activated!
